# Kitchen Screen

The kitchen screen helps kitchen staff track and prepare a branch's orders.

## Language

**Focused Order**:
The order selected by kitchen staff for detailed viewing and preparation actions. Selecting an order does not change its preparation status.
_Avoid_: Current order, active order

**Order Queue**:
The visible collection of open orders from which staff select a focused order.
_Avoid_: Carousel

**New Order**:
An order whose preparation has not started, regardless of whether kitchen staff have viewed it.
_Avoid_: Unseen order

**Unseen Order**:
An arriving order that kitchen staff have not yet opened for detailed viewing.
_Avoid_: New order

**Acknowledged Changes**:
Changes to an order's items that kitchen staff have explicitly confirmed they have reviewed. Viewing the order alone does not acknowledge its changes.

**Reopened Order**:
An order previously marked ready that needs additional preparation after its items change.
